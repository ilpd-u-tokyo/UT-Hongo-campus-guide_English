Walking Tour Spots

STOP 01

Akamon (Red Gate)

赤門

Akamon, or the Red Gate, was built in 1827 during the late Edo period. It originally served as a ceremonial gate for the Maeda clan's residence and is now a designated Important Cultural Property.

STOP 02

Sanshiro Pond

三四郎池

A tranquil pond surrounded by lush greenery, famous for its appearance in Natsume Soseki's novel "Sanshiro".

STOP 03

Hobun Bldg No.2 Archway

法文2号館 アーチ通路

Pass through the iconic gothic archway of Hobun Building No. 2, leading from the Ginkgo Avenue toward Yasuda Auditorium.

STOP 04

Yasuda Auditorium

安田講堂

The symbol of the university, standing prominently at the end of the central avenue.

STOP 05

General Library

総合図書館

An impressive library housing vast historical and modern research collections.

STOP 06

Ginkgo Avenue

イチョウ並木

The main promenade running through the heart of Hongo Campus.

STOP 07 — FINISH

Main Gate / Bus Pickup Point

正門前（バス乗降場所）

The final destination of our tour. Please board the bus waiting along Hongo-dori by 14:50.
